Privacy policy

Woodruff Family · Effective November 12, 2026

Woodruff Family ("the app") is a private app made for our family's own TVs and phones. This policy explains what the app does with information from your Google account and elsewhere.

The short version

  • Your Google data goes only to the Woodruff Family TVs on our home network. We don't run a server that collects it.
  • We never sell your data, use it for ads, or share it with anyone else.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time.

Google account data the app uses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks only for the access it needs for these features:

AccessWhat it's used for
Google Drive (read only)Listing and playing the movies, TV shows, music and photos in your Drive folders on the TV.
Drive app dataSaving the app's own settings in a hidden app folder in your Drive. The app can't see your other files through this.
Google Photos (selected items, read only)Showing the photos and videos you pick in a slideshow on the TV.
Google Calendar (read only)Showing your upcoming events on the TV.
Nest devices (Smart Device Management) and Pub/SubShowing your Nest camera and doorbell feeds and receiving doorbell and motion events so the TV and your phone can alert you.

The app reads this data to show it to you on the TV. It doesn't change or delete anything in your Google account, apart from its own settings in the Drive app folder.

Where data is stored

Sign-in tokens and other secrets are stored on the TV, encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore. Google data is fetched when it's needed and kept on the TV only to show it (for example, a cache of artwork and file lists). The sign-in page at blakew316.github.io passes Google's one-time code straight to the TV on your home network and never sees your tokens.

Other services the app uses

  • The Movie Database (TMDB): movie and show titles are looked up to get posters and descriptions. No Google account data is sent.
  • Open-Meteo: a place name or location is sent to get the weather forecast.
  • ntfy.sh: lock-screen alerts (such as "someone's at the door") are sent to your phone through a random, private ntfy topic.
  • GitHub: the TV checks GitHub for app updates.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Woodruff Family's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features above, is not transferred to others, is not used for advertising, and is not read by people except with your permission, for security, or where the law requires it.

Removing access and deleting data

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Signing out on the TV deletes your tokens from it. To have everything the app stored about you removed, email us and we'll delete it.

Children

The app is for our household. Children in the family use it under a parent's Google account and supervision.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
