Woodruff Family

Woodruff Family is a private media hub for our family's Android TV and Google TV sets, with a companion page for our phones. It isn't sold or offered to the public.

What it does

  • Plays our own movies, TV shows, music and photos from the TV, USB drives and our Google Drive.
  • Shows a photo slideshow from pictures we pick in Google Photos.
  • Shows our Google Calendar events and the local weather.
  • Shows our Nest cameras and doorbell, and alerts our phones when someone's at the door.
  • Lets a paired phone act as a remote and keyboard for the TV.

Signing in with Google

A family member signs in with their Google account on their phone, and the TV on our home network stores the access it's given. Read the privacy policy for what the app uses and why.
