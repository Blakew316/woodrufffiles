# scrolling and starting a film don't run through the interpreter/JIT. A sideloaded APK isn't
# compiled at install: profileinstaller hands this profile to Android when the app first runs, and
# the TV compiles it when it's idle (usually overnight). Updates from the app itself bring it along
# as a .dm file on Android 9+ (update/Updater.kt), and then it's compiled as they install.
# Compose, Coil and most AndroidX libraries ship their own profiles, which are merged in automatically.
